Who we are

Where design and construction meet

Shetty Infraventures is a design-build studio. We take on architecture, interior design, planning and construction, along with real estate guidance, so you work with one accountable team from the first idea to the day you move in.

Our projects range from private residences to institutional spaces such as canteens, auditoriums and offices. Each starts with the place itself: its light, its climate and the people who will use it.
