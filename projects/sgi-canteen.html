SGI Canteen

Where nature breathes, air flows, and the table becomes the heart of design.

An open, semi-covered dining space organised around mature trees and terracotta jaali screens. Light and air move freely through the pattern, throwing soft, shifting shadows across the day.

Patterned floor tiles, warm earthy tones and generous planting keep the space grounded and honest. It is a canteen designed to feel like a courtyard.

Spaces that breathe  ·  Textures that tell stories
